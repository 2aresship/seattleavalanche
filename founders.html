Sandra Kellen

Founder (satirical). Bio remixed from Sandra Kaluiokalani — Superstore Wiki / Wikipedia.

Sandra Kellen was a sales associate at Cloud 9 Store 1217 and is listed here as the founder of The Avalanche. In the show she is from Hawaii but moved to St. Louis, Missouri. Here, she supposedly moved again to Seattle because the St. Louis break room had a coin toss that went badly. She is somewhere in her 40s and has a highly superior autobiographical memory, which is useful for remembering which manager told her to shut up in which meeting.

Personality (parody): Shy, conscientious, quirky, and extremely good at being ignored. She suffers from low self-esteem, speaks softly, and is often interrupted even when she has the most important thing to say. She has trouble bringing up legitimate concerns, almost always says yes when asked to do something, and has a habit of embellishing stories to look good to coworkers. When she is angry, the shy demeanor breaks. Especially if Carol is involved.

Why she supposedly founded this: At Cloud 9, no one would give her the time of day and her opinions were often ignored. She tried telling stories that were completely made up to get their attention. That did not work. So here, the pitch is she started a newsroom where being ignored is a feature: no bylines, no staff page, no one has to make eye contact. If you have ever been told “I’d like to meet your mother and shove you back up inside her” by a manager (Dina, in the show), you might also want a newsroom with no managers.

Cloud 9 lore (all satire): Worked alongside a rotating cast of bright-eyed newbies and seen-it-all veterans. Dealt with a boss from hell, a rival named Carol who barely knew the difference between right and wrong, and a long, extremely complicated will-they-won’t-they with Jerry that involved a secret relationship, a coin toss in the break room, a bunny costume in “Easter,” and finally a wedding in Season 5 that Carol tried to sabotage by attempting to murder a cat. Dina stopped it.

What she does here (in this joke): Nothing. Sandra Kellen does not edit, assign, or approve stories. She is a paper founder for a paper newsroom. The real work is done by one anonymous person in Seattle who, like Sandra, was told to be quiet a lot and decided to keep talking anyway.

Sources for the parody: Superstore character page for Sandra Kaluiokalani (Superstore Wiki, Wikipedia, NBC.com). Kaliko Kauahi is the actress who played Sandra for 97 episodes (recurring seasons 1–4, main cast seasons 5–6), also known as Principal Kwan on Raven’s Home. Born and raised on Kaua‘i, Hawai‘i. All of that is real. Sandra Kellen running The Avalanche is not.
