What The Avalanche is

One city, one person

Each Avalanche outlet is run by a single person in that city. No team, no office. That keeps the surface area small and the accountability clear. Seattle follows that model. Every future city will too.

Shared core, local reporting

Outlets share the same stack, privacy model, and ethics: no ads, no trackers, privatized drop box, scrubbed on arrival, slow verification, no bylines. Reporting itself is strictly local to the city.
