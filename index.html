How this works

Read the manifesto →
01

You send things

Tips, documents, recordings, or plain questions. Everything comes through channels built to leave no trace. Most stories start this way.

02

Verification begins

Every claim gets documents, corroboration, or both. If it can’t be nailed down, it doesn’t run. No exceptions, no “sources say.”

03

The slow part

Weeks of digging, cross-checking, and legal review. All of it done alone, on purpose. One person answers for every word.

04

Publication & answers

The story goes up with its receipts. Questions get answered on the public answers board or directly if you left a way to reply.
